Claims Coder Agents

A vet's notes become a claim an adjuster can pay the same day, and a person signs off on anything the agents are unsure of.

In design

  1. Upload a PDF or photo of a SOAP note and invoice; agents read it in your browser.
  2. Agents code the diagnosis, map each invoice line and check the policy.
  3. A second agent reviews the first; disagreements wait for a person.
  4. Nothing is denied or paid without a person. A master shutoff stops every agent.

Designer and builder: Elizabeth Hannan, 2026. No backend, no accounts, no tracking; nothing you do here leaves your browser.
